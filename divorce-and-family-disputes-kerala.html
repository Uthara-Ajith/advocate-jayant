Divorce and Family Disputes in Kerala

Understanding the Legal Process

By Advocate Jayant S

Family and matrimonial disputes can involve emotional as well as legal issues. Divorce, maintenance, child custody, domestic disputes and related proceedings may require professional legal assistance.

Divorce Proceedings

The procedure for divorce depends on the circumstances and the law applicable to the parties.

A lawyer can explain the available legal routes and the documents and information required for initiating or defending proceedings.

Mutual Consent Divorce

Where the legal requirements are satisfied and both spouses agree to end the marriage, a mutual-consent procedure may be available under the applicable law.

The requirements and procedure should be confirmed based on the parties' circumstances.

Contested Divorce

Where the spouses do not agree on divorce or related issues, court proceedings may become necessary.

The grounds and procedure depend on the applicable personal or statutory law.

Maintenance and Financial Issues

Financial support and maintenance can become issues in matrimonial proceedings. The applicable law, financial circumstances and facts of the case can affect the relief available.

Child Custody

Where children are involved, custody and related issues are generally considered separately from the interests of the spouses. The applicable legal framework focuses on the child's welfare and the circumstances relevant to the case.

This article contains general information and should not be treated as legal advice for a specific family dispute.
