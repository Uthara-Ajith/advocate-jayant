Motor Accident Claims in Kerala

Documents and Legal Procedure

By Advocate Jayant S

Motor vehicle accidents can result in injuries, property damage, loss of income and other financial consequences. Depending on the circumstances, an injured person or eligible family members may have legal remedies under applicable motor vehicle and compensation laws.

What Is a Motor Accident Claim?

A motor accident claim may involve seeking compensation arising from an accident involving a motor vehicle.

The applicable remedy depends on factors including the nature of the accident, injuries, vehicles involved, insurance coverage and other circumstances.

Important Documents

Documents that may be relevant include:

  • Police records
  • Medical records
  • Hospital bills
  • Medical certificates
  • Vehicle documents
  • Insurance documents
  • Income-related documents
  • Identity documents
  • Photographs or other evidence concerning the accident

The documents required can vary depending on the case.

Medical Evidence

Medical records can be important in establishing the nature and extent of injuries. Keeping hospital records, bills and medical reports can therefore be useful.

MACT Proceedings

Motor Accident Claims Tribunal proceedings involve legal and evidentiary requirements. A lawyer can assist with preparing the claim, presenting documents and representing the claimant during proceedings.

Why Legal Advice Can Help

Determining the appropriate claim and presenting supporting evidence can involve legal and factual issues.

If you or a family member is involved in a motor accident in Kerala, professional legal advice can help you understand the available remedies.

This article is general information and does not constitute an assessment of any individual accident claim.
