Legal Services for Businesses

Contracts, Disputes and Compliance

By Advocate Jayant S

Businesses regularly enter into agreements, transactions and commercial relationships. Legal advice can help businesses understand contractual obligations and address disputes when they arise.

Business Contracts

Contracts may cover services, supply arrangements, employment, partnerships, leases, payments and other commercial relationships.

Before signing an important agreement, businesses may benefit from reviewing provisions concerning:

  • Responsibilities of each party
  • Payment terms
  • Termination
  • Confidentiality
  • Liability
  • Dispute resolution
  • Applicable law

Business Disputes

Commercial disputes may involve non-payment, breach of contract, service issues, partnership disagreements or other claims.

A lawyer can examine the relevant agreement and documents and advise on possible remedies.

Legal Notices and Recovery

Where a business is owed money or believes that a contractual obligation has been breached, a legal notice may be one of the steps considered.

The appropriate approach depends on the facts and applicable law.

Preventive Legal Advice

Legal assistance is not limited to disputes. Reviewing agreements before execution can help identify unclear provisions and potential legal issues.

This article provides general information and is not a substitute for advice concerning a specific business transaction or dispute.
